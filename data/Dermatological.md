| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Atopic Dermatitis (Eczema) | L20.9 | Intense itching (pruritus), dry scaly skin, red to brownish-gray patches (flexural folds). | Skin thickening (lichenification), oozing or crusting lesions, raw/sensitive skin from scratching. | Family history of asthma or allergic rhinitis (atopic triad), environmental allergens, dry weather, harsh soaps. | Severe localized pain, rapid spreading redness, pus-filled blisters, fever (secondary bacterial infection/Eczema Herpeticum). |
| Psoriasis (Plaque Psoriasis) | L40.0 | Well-demarcated red skin patches covered with thick silver-white scales, localized itching, burning sensation. | Dry cracked skin that may bleed, stiff or painful joints (psoriatic arthritis), nail pitting or discoloration. | Autoimmune predisposition, severe mental stress, skin trauma (Koebner phenomenon), cold weather, beta-blocker usage. | Erythrodermic flare (redness covering >90% of body), generalized pustules with fever, severe chills, rapid heart rate. |
| Acne Vulgaris | L70.0 | Comedones (blackheads and whiteheads), inflamed red papules, pustules on face, chest, and upper back. | Painful deep cysts or nodules, hyperpigmentation, permanent scarring (pitting). | Hormonal fluctuations (puberty, PCOS), high sebum production, Cutibacterium acnes overgrowth, comedogenic cosmetics. | Sudden widespread outbreak of painful ulcerative nodules with high fever and joint pain (Acne Fulminans). |
| Rosacea | L71.9 | Persistent facial redness (erythema), flushing, visible broken blood vessels (telangiectasia), red bumps. | Stinging or burning sensation, swollen red nose with thickened skin (rhinophyma), watery or irritated eyes (ocular rosacea). | Fair skin tone, age 30–50, exposure to sun/wind, spicy foods, alcohol, hot beverages, emotional stress. | Severe eye pain, light sensitivity (photophobia), blurred vision (ocular involvement requiring ophthalmology evaluation). |
| Cellulitis | L03.90 | Rapidly spreading skin redness, localized warmth, swelling, tender or painful skin touch. | Red streaks expanding from the site, swollen lymph nodes, general malaise, skin blistering. | Skin barrier breakdown (cuts, insect bites, surgical wounds, athlete's foot), venous insufficiency, lymphedema, diabetes. | High fever, chills, rapid expansion of redness (>1 inch/hour), purple skin discoloration, confusion, hypotension. |
| Contact Dermatitis | L25.9 | Itchy red rash, localized swelling, clear fluid-filled blisters (vesicles) at the contact site. | Burning sensation, dry cracking skin, scaling, hyperpigmentation following chronic exposure. | Exposure to allergens (nickel, poison ivy, fragrances) or irritants (soaps, solvents, bleach, industrial chemicals). | Rash affecting >30% of body, facial swelling, blisters near eyes/mouth, signs of secondary infection (yellow pus, warmth). |
| Melanoma (Malignant Melanoma) | C43.9 | Asymmetrical mole or spot, irregular borders, multiple/uneven colors (black, brown, blue), diameter >6mm. | Evolving mole (changing size, shape, color), itching, oozing, bleeding from a spot, newly elevated bump. | Excessive UV radiation exposure (tanning beds/sunburns), fair skin/light hair, >50 common moles, family history. | Rapidly growing firm dark nodule, spontaneous ulceration/bleeding, newly enlarged firm lymph nodes nearby. |
| Urticaria (Hives) | L50.9 | Raised, intense itchy red or skin-colored welts (wheals) that blanch (turn white) under pressure. | Burning or stinging pain, individual lesions resolving within 24 hours while new ones appear elsewhere. | Allergic reactions (foods, drugs, insect stings), viral infections, physical stimuli (heat, cold, friction, pressure). | Facial swelling (angioedema), swelling of lips, tongue, or throat, difficulty breathing or swallowing (Anaphylaxis). |
