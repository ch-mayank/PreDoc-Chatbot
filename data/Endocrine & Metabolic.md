| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus | E11.9 | Increased thirst (polydipsia), frequent urination (polyuria), increased hunger (polyphagia), unexplained weight loss. | Blurred vision, fatigue, tingling/numbness in hands or feet (neuropathy), slow-healing sores, frequent infections. | Insulin resistance, obesity, sedentary lifestyle, family history, age >45, gestational diabetes history. | Extreme lethargy, fruity-smelling breath, rapid deep breathing (Kussmaul), confusion, glucose >600 mg/dL (HHS/DKA). |
| Hypothyroidism | E03.9 | Fatigue, unexplained weight gain, cold intolerance, dry skin, constipation. | Muscle weakness, joint stiffness, thinning hair, hoarseness, memory impairment, elevated cholesterol. | Autoimmune conditions (Hashimoto's thyroiditis), iodine deficiency, past thyroid surgery/radiation, female sex. | Extreme hypothermia, severe confusion or unresponsiveness, slow heart rate, puffiness across body (Myxedema Coma). |
| Hyperthyroidism (Graves' Disease) | E05.9 / E05.0 | Unintended weight loss, rapid or irregular heartbeat (tachycardia/arrhythmia), heat intolerance, anxiety/irritability. | Hand tremors, increased sweating, frequent bowel movements, enlarged thyroid gland (goiter), sleep disturbance. | Autoimmune predisposition (Graves' disease), thyroid nodules, excessive iodine intake, female sex. | High fever, extreme agitation/delirium, rapid heart rate >140 bpm, persistent vomiting, yellowing skin (Thyroid Storm). |
| Primary Adrenal Insufficiency (Addison's Disease) | E27.1 | Chronic fatigue, muscle weakness, loss of appetite, weight loss, hyperpigmentation (darkened skin on knuckles/palms/scars). | Low blood pressure (orthostatic hypotension), salt craving, nausea, diarrhea, joint pain, hypoglycemia. | Autoimmune destruction of adrenal cortex, sudden cessation of long-term corticosteroid therapy, infections (TB). | Severe abdominal/lower back pain, persistent vomiting, severe dehydration, sudden profound low blood pressure (Addisonian Crisis). |
| Cushing's Syndrome | E24.9 | Progressive weight gain in upper body/face ("moon face", "buffalo hump"), purple stretch marks (striae) on abdomen. | Fragile skin that bruises easily, muscle weakness in thighs/shoulders, high blood pressure, bone loss (osteoporosis). | Exogenous corticosteroid overuse, pituitary ACTH-secreting tumor, adrenal gland tumors. | Sudden severe back pain (vertebral compression fracture), sudden severe high blood pressure, signs of severe systemic infection. |
| Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) | E28.2 | Irregular or absent menstrual periods, excess facial/body hair (hirsutism), severe acne. | Male-pattern baldness, weight gain or difficulty losing weight, darkened skin patches (acanthosis nigricans), ovarian cysts. | Insulin resistance, chronic low-grade inflammation, genetics, high androgen levels. | Sudden severe lower abdominal or pelvic pain (ovarian torsion or ruptured ovarian cyst). |
| Hyperparathyroidism | E21.3 | Recurrent kidney stones, bone pain, joint pain, abdominal pain ("bones, stones, abdominal groans, psychiatric overtones"). | Frequent urination, muscle weakness, nausea, constipation, fatigue, depression or memory loss. | Parathyroid adenoma, chronic kidney disease (secondary), vitamin D deficiency, radiation exposure to neck. | Severe confusion, lethargy, muscle spasms, intractable vomiting, irregular heart rhythm (severe Hypercalcemia). |
| Metabolic Syndrome | E88.81 | Increased waist circumference (abdominal obesity), elevated blood pressure, high fasting blood sugar. | Elevated triglycerides, low HDL cholesterol, skin tags, acanthosis nigricans, general fatigue. | Physical inactivity, high-carbohydrate/ultra-processed diet, insulin resistance, genetics, age. | Chest pain, sudden facial/arm numbness, severe shortness of breath (acute cardiovascular or cerebrovascular event). |
