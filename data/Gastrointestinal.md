Document Purpose: Baseline clinical context for Healthcare AI Assistant symptom matching.

| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD) | K21.9 | Heartburn (burning sensation in chest behind breastbone), acid regurgitation, upper abdominal pain. | Difficulty swallowing (dysphagia), chest pain, chronic cough, laryngitis, feeling of a lump in throat (globus). | Obesity, hiatal hernia, pregnancy, smoking, fatty/spicy foods, caffeine, late-night meals. | Persistent difficulty swallowing or painful swallowing (odynophagia), unexplained weight loss, vomiting blood. |
| Peptic Ulcer Disease (PUD) | K27.9 | Burning stomach pain (worse on empty stomach or at night), feeling of fullness, bloating, belching. | Intolerance to fatty foods, heartburn, nausea, fatty or dark stools, early satiety. | Helicobacter pylori infection, long-term NSAID use (aspirin, ibuprofen), smoking, severe physiological stress. | Vomiting blood or coffee-ground material, black tarry stools (melena), sudden severe sharp abdominal pain (perforation). |
| Acute Appendicitis | K35.80 | Periumbilical pain migrating to the right lower quadrant (RLQ), loss of appetite, low-grade fever. | Nausea, vomiting, abdominal swelling, localized McBurney's point tenderness, inability to pass gas. | Age 10–30, male sex, obstruction of the appendiceal lumen (fecaliths, lymphoid hyperplasia). | Sudden relief of pain followed by worsening generalized abdominal rigidity/guarding and high fever (ruptured appendix). |
| Inflammatory Bowel Disease (Crohn's / Ulcerative Colitis) | K50.9 / K51.9 | Persistent diarrhea (often with blood/mucus), crampy abdominal pain, weight loss, chronic fatigue. | Fever, reduced appetite, mouth sores, joint pain, skin lesions, delayed growth in children. | Family history, autoimmune dysregulation, smoking (risk factor for Crohn's, protective/modifying in UC), Western diet. | Severe profuse bloody diarrhea (>10 episodes/day), high fever, severe abdominal distension, signs of bowel obstruction. |
| Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) | K58.9 | Abdominal pain/cramping related to defecation, changes in stool frequency or consistency (diarrhea/constipation). | Bloating, gas, feeling of incomplete bowel movement, mucus in stool. | Stress, history of severe gut infection (post-infectious IBS), gut dysbiosis, female sex, anxiety/depression. | Rectal bleeding, persistent nocturnal diarrhea, unexplained weight loss, onset of symptoms after age 50. |
| Acute Diverticulitis | K57.32 | Constant severe pain in the lower left quadrant (LLQ), fever, nausea, abdominal tenderness. | Constipation or diarrhea, bloating, chills, elevated white blood cell count. | Low-fiber diet, aging (>50), obesity, sedentary lifestyle, smoking, nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs. | Severe rebound tenderness, rigid board-like abdomen, high fever, inability to keep liquids down (perforation/peritonitis). |
| Acute Cholecystitis (Gallbladder Inflammation) | K81.0 | Sudden severe pain in the upper right abdomen (RUQ) or epigastrium, radiating to right shoulder/back. | Nausea, vomiting, fever, chills, tenderness over RUQ (positive Murphy's sign). | Female sex, age >40, obesity, rapid weight loss, pregnancy, gallstones (cholelithiasis). | Jaundice (yellowing of eyes/skin), high fever with rigors, severe hypotension, confusion (gallstone pancreatitis/cholangitis). |
| Acute Pancreatitis | K85.90 | Severe upper abdominal pain radiating straight through to the back, nausea, persistent vomiting. | Abdominal tenderness/swelling, rapid pulse, fever, bluish skin discoloration around navel (Cullen's sign) or flanks. | Heavy alcohol consumption, gallstones, high blood triglyceride levels, certain medications, abdominal trauma. | Persistent severe vomiting with inability to retain fluids, rapid shallow breathing, low blood pressure, cold clammy skin. |
