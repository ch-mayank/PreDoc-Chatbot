Category: Musculoskeletal System
Source Standard: WHO / CDC / ICD-10 Clinical Reference Data
Document Purpose: Baseline clinical context for Healthcare AI Assistant symptom matching.

| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Osteoarthritis (OA) | M19.90 | Joint pain that worsens with activity, morning stiffness lasting <30 minutes, joint tenderness. | Reduced range of motion, grating sensation (crepitus), joint swelling, bony enlargements (nodes). | Age, joint trauma or overuse, obesity, genetics, female sex. | Sudden locking of a joint, severe unmanaged pain, inability to bear weight, signs of joint infection (fever, redness). |
| Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA) | M06.9 | Symmetric joint pain and swelling (especially hands/wrists), prolonged morning stiffness (>1 hour), fatigue. | Low-grade fever, weight loss, rheumatoid nodules under skin, joint deformities over time. | Autoimmune predisposition, genetics, smoking, female sex, age 40–60. | High fever with severe painful joint swelling, sudden chest pain (pleuritis/pericarditis), eye inflammation (scleritis). |
| Fibromyalgia | M79.7 | Widespread musculoskeletal pain, chronic fatigue, widespread tender points, sleep disturbances. | "Fibro fog" (cognitive/memory issues), headaches, numbness or tingling in hands/feet, irritable bowel symptoms. | Genetics, physical or emotional trauma, chronic stress, female sex. | Severe depressive crisis, profound functional disability, completely incapacitating widespread pain flares. |
| Gout | M10.9 | Sudden, severe attacks of sharp pain, swelling, redness, and tenderness in a single joint (often base of big toe). | Warmth and intense redness over affected joint, lingering discomfort, limited movement during flares. | High purine diet, alcohol consumption, obesity, male sex, hypertension, kidney disease, genetics. | Excruciating pain accompanied by high fever and chills, hot/dusky red joint appearance (Septic Arthritis rule-out). |
| Osteoporosis | M81.0 | Generally **asymptomatic** ("silent disease") until a fracture occurs. | Loss of height over time, stooped posture (dowager's hump), back pain caused by fractured or collapsed vertebra. | Aging, postmenopausal status (estrogen loss), low calcium/vitamin D, sedentary lifestyle, smoking, corticosteroid use. | Sudden severe back pain with inability to stand or walk, acute localized spine tenderness (vertebral compression fracture). |
| Low Back Pain (Lumbar Strain / Disc Disease) | M54.50 / M51.26 | Dull or sharp lower back ache, muscle stiffness in lumbar region, pain aggravated by movement. | Muscle spasms, localized tenderness, restricted range of motion. | Heavy lifting, poor ergonomics, sedentary lifestyle, obesity, sudden twisting movements. | Radiating leg pain/numbness below the knee (sciatica), progressive leg weakness, sudden loss of bowel or bladder control (Cauda Equina Syndrome). |
| Carpal Tunnel Syndrome | G56.00 | Numbness, tingling (pins and needles), or burning in the thumb, index, and middle fingers. | Weakness in the hand, dropping objects, radiating shock-like sensations up the arm, nighttime awakenings due to pain. | Repetitive hand/wrist motions, wrist anatomy, pregnancy, diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, hypothyroidism. | Sudden total loss of thumb muscle strength, permanent loss of sensation in fingers, severe unrelenting hand atrophy. |
| Ankylosing Spondylitis | M45.9 | Chronic pain and stiffness in lower back and hips (worsening in the morning or after inactivity). | Reduced spinal flexibility, fatigue, inflammation of eyes (uveitis), pain where tendons/ligaments attach to bones. | Genetics (HLA-B27 marker), male sex, young adulthood (onset typically teens to 30s). | Sudden severe spinal pain following minor trauma, acute eye pain with severe vision blurring/redness, breathing difficulty due to chest wall restriction. |
