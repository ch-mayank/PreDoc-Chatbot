Category: Cardiovascular System
Source Standard: WHO / CDC Clinical Reference Data
Document Purpose: Baseline clinical context for Healthcare AI Assistant symptom matching.

| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Coronary Artery Disease (CAD) | I25.1 | Angina (chest pain, pressure, or tightness), shortness of breath, general fatigue. | Neck, jaw, or upper back pain; heartburn-like discomfort; arm weakness. | High blood pressure, high LDL cholesterol, smoking, physical inactivity, diabetes. | Crushing chest pressure lasting >10 minutes, pain radiating to left arm/jaw, fainting. |
| Myocardial Infarction (Heart Attack) | I21.9 | Severe chest pain/pressure, shortness of breath, cold sweats, nausea/vomiting. | Lightheadedness, extreme fatigue, discomfort radiating to back/shoulder/jaw. | Pre-existing CAD, sudden physical exertion, high stress, history of hypertension. | Sudden onset chest crushing, blue-tinged lips/fingers, collapse/loss of consciousness. |
| Heart Failure (CHF) | I50.9 | Shortness of breath during activity or lying flat (orthopnea), swelling in legs/ankles/feet (edema), persistent fatigue. | Persistent cough or wheezing with white/pink blood-tinged phlegm, rapid weight gain (fluid retention), abdominal swelling. | Previous heart attack, high blood pressure, valvular heart disease, cardiomyopathy, diabetes. | Sudden extreme breathlessness, coughing up pink foam, rapid or irregular heartbeat with fainting. |
| Atrial Fibrillation (AFib / Arrhythmia) | I48.91 | Heart palpitations (racing, fluttering, or pounding heart), fatigue, lightheadedness. | Dizziness, reduced ability to exercise, general weakness, mild chest pain. | High blood pressure, heavy alcohol use, sleep apnea, thyroid issues, older age. | Chest pain with racing heart rate (>150 bpm) at rest, sudden onset of confusion or slurred speech. |
| Stroke (Cerebrovascular Event) | I63.9 | Sudden weakness or numbness on one side of the face/arm/leg, sudden confusion or speech difficulty. | Sudden vision loss or double vision, loss of balance/dizziness, sudden severe headache with no known cause. | High blood pressure, AFib, high cholesterol, smoking, diabetes. | FAST: Facial drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency services. |
| Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD) | I73.9 | Leg pain or cramping while walking (intermittent claudication) that disappears with rest. | Coldness in lower leg/foot, numbness/weakness in legs, slow-healing sores on toes/feet/legs, shiny leg skin. | Smoking, diabetes, high cholesterol, age over 50, high blood pressure. | Sudden severe leg pain, pale or bluish leg skin, cold leg with loss of pulse (acute limb ischemia). |
| Hypertension (High Blood Pressure) | I10 | Often asymptomatic ("silent killer"). | Morning headaches, dull head pressure, nosebleeds, vision changes, ringing in ears (tinnitus). | High sodium diet, stress, genetics, lack of physical activity, obesity. | Systolic > 180 or Diastolic > 120 with severe headache, chest pain, or visual disturbances (Hypertensive Crisis). |
| Infective Endocarditis / Pericarditis | I33.0 / I30.9 | Sharp, stabbing chest pain (often improves when sitting up/leaning forward), fever, chills. | Heart murmur, fatigue, muscle/joint aches, swelling in legs or abdomen, persistent dry cough. | Bacterial infections, recent dental/surgical procedures, IV drug use, damaged heart valves. | High fever accompanied by severe chest pain, extreme breathlessness, or confusion. |
