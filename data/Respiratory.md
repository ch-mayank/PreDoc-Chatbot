Source Standard: WHO / CDC / ICD-10 Clinical Reference Data
Document Purpose: Baseline clinical context for Healthcare AI Assistant symptom matching.

| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Asthma | J45.909 | Wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, coughing (especially at night or early morning). | Rapid breathing, inability to finish sentences in one breath, use of neck muscles to breathe. | Allergies (pollen, dust mites, pet dander), exercise, cold air, respiratory infections, smoke, family history. | Severe bluish lips or fingernails, extreme difficulty breathing, peak flow reading in the red zone, unresponsiveness (Asthma Attack). |
| Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) | J44.9 | Ongoing chronic cough ("smoker's cough"), daily sputum/mucus production, progressive shortness of breath during exertion. | Wheezing, chest tightness, frequent respiratory infections, fatigue, mild ankle/foot swelling. | Long-term smoking or exposure to secondhand smoke, occupational dust/chemical fumes, alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency. | Sudden severe breathlessness at rest, confusion or blue lips/fingers, severe drop in blood oxygen levels (COPD Exacerbation). |
| Pneumonia (Bacterial / Viral) | J18.9 | Cough producing greenish, yellow, or bloody mucus; fever, sweating, and shaking chills; sharp chest pain when breathing/coughing. | Shortness of breath at rest, fatigue, nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea (especially in viral/legionella cases). | Advanced age or very young age, weakened immune system, smoking, chronic medical conditions (asthma, heart disease). | Confusion or altered mental state, rapid breathing (>30 breaths/min), low blood pressure, bluish skin/lips (Severe Sepsis/Hypoxia). |
| Acute Bronchitis | J20.9 | Persistent cough lasting 1 to 3 weeks (often producing clear, yellow, or green mucus), mild chest discomfort, fatigue. | Low-grade fever, slight body aches or headache, mild sore throat, nasal congestion. | Viral infections (cold/flu), cigarette smoking, exposure to air pollutants, dust, or chemical fumes. | High persistent fever (>102°F/39°C), coughing up blood, shortness of breath, symptoms lasting longer than 3 weeks. |
| Pulmonary Embolism (PE) | I26.99 | Sudden unexplained shortness of breath, sharp chest pain (worsening with deep breath or cough), rapid heart rate. | Coughing up blood, lightheadedness, dizziness, fainting, anxiety, sweating, leg swelling/pain (DVT). | Prolonged immobility (long flights/bed rest), surgery, history of DVT/clots, cancer, pregnancy, smoking. | Sudden crushing chest pain, catastrophic drop in blood pressure, severe blue discoloration, rapid loss of consciousness. |
| Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) | G47.33 | Loud chronic snoring, episodes of breathing cessation during sleep witnessed by others, waking up with a dry mouth or sore throat. | Excessive daytime sleepiness (somnolence), morning headaches, difficulty concentrating, irritability, high blood pressure. | Obesity, large neck circumference, narrowed airway, male sex, older age, family history, smoking. | Extreme nocturnal choking or gasping waking the patient up with severe chest pain or racing heart rate. |
| Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis (IPF) | J84.112 | Unexplained chronic dry, hacking cough; progressive shortness of breath especially during physical activity. | Gradual unintended weight loss, fatigue, aching joints and muscles, clubbing (widening/rounding) of fingertips. | Age over 50, male sex, history of cigarette smoking, occupational exposure to dust (silica/metal), genetic predisposition. | Rapidly worsening severe shortness of breath, sudden acute respiratory failure, blue lips or skin. |
| Pulmonary Hypertension | I27.20 | Shortness of breath during routine activity, fatigue, dizziness or fainting spells (syncope), chest pressure or pain. | Swelling (edema) in ankles, legs, and abdomen; racing pulse or heart palpitations; bluish lips/skin (cyanosis). | Left-sided heart disease, chronic lung diseases (COPD/fibrosis), blood clots in lungs, connective tissue disorders. | Severe chest pain at rest, fainting while standing or exercising, extreme difficulty breathing, signs of right heart failure. |
