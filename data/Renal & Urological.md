Document Purpose: Baseline clinical context for Healthcare AI Assistant symptom matching.

| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) | N18.9 | Fatigue, swelling in feet and ankles (edema), changes in urination frequency (especially at night), nausea. | Itchy skin, muscle cramps, loss of appetite, shortness of breath (fluid overload), metallic taste in mouth. | Diabetes, high blood pressure, cardiovascular disease, family history of kidney failure, age >60. | Sudden severe decrease in urine output, extreme shortness of breath, confusion, severe chest pain (uremic crisis/pericarditis). |
| Nephrolithiasis (Kidney Stones) | N20.0 | Severe, sharp pain in the back, side, lower abdomen, or groin (renal colic); blood in urine (hematuria). | Nausea, vomiting, frequent urge to urinate, burning sensation during urination, cloudy or foul-smelling urine. | Dehydration, low fluid intake, family history, high-sodium/high-protein diet, obesity, gout. | Excruciating unrelenting pain unresponsive to medication, high fever with shaking chills, inability to pass any urine (obstructed ureter with infection). |
| Urinary Tract Infection (UTI) / Cystitis | N30.90 | Strong persistent urge to urinate, burning sensation during urination, passing frequent small amounts of urine. | Urine that appears cloudy, red, bright pink, or strong-smelling; pelvic pressure; low-grade fever. | Female sex, sexual activity, certain types of birth control, menopause, suppressed immune system, urinary catheter. | High fever with shaking chills, severe lower back/flank pain (pyelonephritis), confusion or delirium (especially in elderly). |
| Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH) | N40.0 | Frequent or urgent need to urinate, increased urination at night (nocturia), weak urine stream, dribbling at end of urination. | Difficulty starting a urine stream, inability to completely empty the bladder, recurrent UTIs. | Aging (men over 50), family history, obesity, type 2 diabetes, sedentary lifestyle. | Complete inability to urinate (acute urinary retention), severe lower abdominal pain, bladder distension. |
| Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) | N17.9 | Decreased urine output (oliguria), fluid retention causing swelling in legs/ankles, fatigue, shortness of breath. | Confusion, nausea, weakness, irregular heartbeat, chest pain or pressure (in severe cases). | Major surgery, severe dehydration, blood loss, sepsis, use of nephrotoxic medications (NSAIDs, contrast dyes). | Complete cessation of urine production (anuria), severe altered mental state, pulmonary edema, seizures. |
| Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD) | Q61.3 | High blood pressure, pain or tenderness in the back or sides, blood in the urine, feeling of fullness in abdomen. | Frequent kidney stones, kidney infections, urinary tract infections, headaches, abdominal enlargement. | Genetic inheritance (autosomal dominant or recessive family history), mutation of PKD1 or PKD2 genes. | Sudden severe sharp pain in the side or abdomen (cyst rupture or hemorrhage), high fever with severe flank pain. |
| Glomerulonephritis | N05.9 | Pink or cola-colored urine from red blood cells (hematuria), foamy urine due to excess protein (proteinuria), high blood pressure. | Fluid retention (edema) evident in face, hands, feet, and abdomen; fatigue from mild anemia. | Strep infections, viral infections (HIV, Hepatitis B/C), autoimmune diseases (lupus, Goodpasture syndrome). | Rapidly worsening high blood pressure, acute severe shortness of breath, sudden drop in urine output, confusion. |
| Interstitial Cystitis (Bladder Pain Syndrome) | N30.10 | Chronic pelvic pain, persistent urgent need to urinate, frequent urination throughout the day and night. | Pain or discomfort during sexual intercourse, pressure or tenderness in the bladder area as it fills. | Female sex, history of chronic pain disorders (fibromyalgia, irritable bowel syndrome), bladder trauma. | Severe, unrelenting pelvic or bladder pain accompanied by blood clots in urine or high fever (sign of concurrent infection). |
