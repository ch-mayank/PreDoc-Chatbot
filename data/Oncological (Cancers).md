Category: Oncological System
Source Standard: WHO / CDC / ICD-10 Clinical Reference Data
Document Purpose: Baseline clinical context for Healthcare AI Assistant symptom matching.

| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Lung Cancer (Non-Small / Small Cell) | C34.90 | Persistent cough that worsens, coughing up blood (hemoptysis), chest pain, shortness of breath, hoarseness. | Unexplained weight loss, recurring respiratory infections (bronchitis/pneumonia), bone pain, fatigue. | Smoking (primary risk factor), secondhand smoke, radon gas exposure, asbestos, family history. | Massive hemoptysis, sudden severe chest pain, acute respiratory distress, neurological changes (brain metastasis). |
| Breast Cancer | C50.919 | New lump or mass in the breast or underarm, change in breast size or shape, skin dimpling or puckering. | Inverted nipple, nipple discharge (other than breast milk), red or flaky skin over the nipple/breast. | Female sex, aging, genetic mutations (BRCA1/BRCA2), family history, radiation exposure, hormonal factors. | Rapidly swelling, hot, red breast with pitting skin (Inflammatory Breast Cancer), severe bone pain. |
| Colorectal Cancer | C18.9 | Change in bowel habits (persistent diarrhea or constipation), blood in stool (bright red or dark black), cramping. | Feeling that bowel doesn't empty completely, weakness, fatigue, unexplained iron-deficiency anemia, weight loss. | Age >50, family history, inflammatory bowel disease, polyps, high-fat/low-fiber diet, physical inactivity. | Complete bowel obstruction, severe unrelenting abdominal distension, acute severe rectal hemorrhage. |
| Prostate Cancer | C61 | Trouble urinating, decreased force in the stream of urine, blood in urine or semen, pelvic discomfort. | Bone pain (if metastasized), erectile dysfunction, unexplained weight loss, fatigue. | Age (65+), family history, African-American ancestry, genetic mutations (BRCA changes), obesity. | Complete inability to urinate (acute urinary retention), sudden severe lower back/spine pain with leg weakness (spinal cord compression). |
| Skin Cancer (Melanoma & Non-Melanoma) | C43.9 / C44.9 | Changes in existing moles (ABCDE criteria: Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Color variation, Diameter >6mm, Evolving), new spots. | Sore that does not heal, bleeding or crusting skin lesion, smooth/waxy or firm red lumps. | Excessive UV radiation (sun exposure/tanning beds), fair skin, history of sunburns, numerous moles, genetics. | Rapidly enlarging, bleeding, ulcerated nodule; hard swollen regional lymph nodes. |
| Leukemia (Acute / Chronic) | C95.90 | Fatigue, generalized weakness, pale skin, frequent infections, unexplained fevers or night sweats. | Easy bruising or bleeding (nosebleeds, bleeding gums), petechiae (tiny red spots on skin), bone/joint pain, swollen lymph nodes. | Genetic disorders (Down syndrome), exposure to radiation/chemicals (benzene), smoking, family history. | Severe uncontrollable bleeding, dangerously low white blood cell count with high fever (neutropenic sepsis), confusion. |
| Lymphoma (Hodgkin / Non-Hodgkin) | C81.90 / C85.90 | Painless swelling of lymph nodes in neck, armpits, or groin; persistent fatigue; recurring fevers; drenching night sweats. | Unexplained weight loss, itchy skin, persistent cough, abdominal fullness, reduced tolerance to alcohol. | Compromised immune system, viral infections (EBV, HIV), age, family history, exposure to certain chemicals. | Rapidly enlarging obstructive mass compromising airways or major blood vessels, severe spinal cord compression. |
| Pancreatic Cancer | C25.9 | Upper abdominal pain radiating to the back, yellowing of skin and eyes (jaundice), unexplained weight loss, loss of appetite. | Dark urine, pale greasy stools, newly diagnosed diabetes, blood clots, severe fatigue, depression. | Smoking, obesity, family history of pancreatic cancer, chronic pancreatitis, genetic mutations (BRCA2, Lynch syndrome). | Severe unrelenting abdominal and back pain, intractable nausea and vomiting, acute cholangitis with high fever. |
