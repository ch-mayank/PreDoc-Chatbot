Source Standard: WHO / CDC / ICD-10 Clinical Reference Data
Document Purpose: Baseline clinical context for Healthcare AI Assistant symptom matching.

| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Alzheimer's Disease / Dementia | G30.9 | Memory loss that disrupts daily life, difficulty planning or solving problems, confusion with time/place. | Misplacing items, poor judgment, withdrawal from social activities, personality/mood changes. | Age (65+), family history, genetics (APOE-e4 gene), cardiovascular disease, head trauma. | Sudden severe confusion, inability to swallow or recognize loved ones, complete loss of motor control. |
| Parkinson's Disease | G20 | Resting tremor (shaking in hands/fingers), slowed movement (bradykinesia), rigid muscles, postural instability. | Shuffling gait, soft speech (hypophonia), loss of automatic movements, mask-like facial expression, depression. | Age, genetics, exposure to toxins/pesticides, male sex. | Sudden severe inability to move (akinetic crisis), severe swallowing difficulties leading to aspiration pneumonia. |
| Multiple Sclerosis (MS) | G35 | Numbness or tingling in limbs, fatigue, electric-shock sensations with neck movements (Lhermitte's sign), vision loss. | Muscle stiffness/spasms, balance issues, dizziness, bladder/bowel dysfunction, cognitive changes. | Autoimmune predisposition, Epstein-Barr virus exposure, smoking, vitamin D deficiency, female sex. | Sudden acute loss of vision, severe progressive limb paralysis, acute cognitive unresponsiveness. |
| Migraine (with/without Aura) | G43.909 | Throbbing or pulsing moderate-to-severe headache (usually one-sided), sensitivity to light and sound, nausea. | Vomiting, blurred vision, sensory disturbances (aura like flashing lights or tingling lines before attack). | Family history, hormonal changes (estrogen fluctuations), stress, lack of sleep, specific foods/triggers. | "Worst headache of your life" (thunderclap headache), sudden fever, stiff neck, double vision, confusion. |
| Epilepsy / Seizure Disorders | G40.909 | Recurrent unprovoked seizures, temporary confusion, staring spells, uncontrollable jerking movements. | Loss of consciousness or awareness, cognitive or psychological symptoms (fear, anxiety, deja vu). | Genetics, brain injury, stroke, brain infections (meningitis), prenatal brain damage, high fever in children. | Status epilepticus (continuous seizure lasting >5 minutes), trouble breathing after seizure, repeated seizures without recovery. |
| Essential Tremor | G25.0 | Involuntary, rhythmic shaking (primarily in hands and fingers when performing tasks like writing or drinking). | Tremors in voice, head, or chin; balance issues (rarely); tremors worsening with stress or caffeine. | Family history (autosomal dominant trait), advanced age. | Sudden massive escalation of tremors preventing basic self-care, accompanied by rigidity or cognitive decline. |
| Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) | G12.21 | Progressive muscle weakness in hands/feet, twitching (fasciculations) and cramping in muscles, slurred speech. | Difficulty swallowing (dysphagia), trouble breathing, emotional lability (uncontrolled laughing/crying). | Genetics (familial ALS), age (40–60), military service (exposure risk), male sex. | Acute respiratory distress, sudden inability to breathe or swallow liquids (diaphragmatic failure). |
| Trigeminal Neuralgia | G50.0 | Sudden, severe, brief shooting or electric-shock facial pain triggered by touch, chewing, talking, or brushing teeth. | Persistent aching or burning sensation in the jaw, cheek, or gums before or between intense pain flares. | Compression of the trigeminal nerve by a blood vessel, multiple sclerosis, aging, female sex. | Constant, unremitting facial pain unresponsive to standard medications, total inability to eat or drink due to pain triggers. |
