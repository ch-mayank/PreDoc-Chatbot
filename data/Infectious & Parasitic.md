Source Standard: WHO / CDC Clinical Reference Data
Document Purpose: Baseline clinical context for Healthcare AI Assistant symptom matching.

| Disease / Condition | ICD-10 Code | Primary / Common Symptoms | Secondary / Severe Symptoms | Risk Factors & Trigger Context | Urgent Warning Signs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Malaria (Plasmodium falciparum / vivax) | B50.9 / B51.9 | High fever with shaking chills, drenching sweats, headaches, muscle aches (myalgia), nausea. | Jaundice (yellowing of eyes/skin), anemia, dark urine, enlarged spleen (splenomegaly), severe fatigue. | Travel to endemic tropical regions, mosquito bites (Anopheles vector), lack of chemoprophylaxis. | Confusion, seizures, dark/black urine (blackwater fever), severe respiratory distress, shock (Cerebral Malaria). |
| Influenza (Flu) | J11.1 | Sudden high fever, dry cough, severe body aches, sore throat, extreme fatigue, headache. | Nasal congestion, chest discomfort, vomiting/diarrhea (more common in children), persistent weakness. | Exposure to respiratory droplets, crowded indoor settings, winter seasons, lack of annual vaccination. | Difficulty breathing, blue lips or face, persistent chest pain/pressure, confusion, severe dizziness. |
| Tuberculosis (Pulmonary TB) | A15.0 | Persistent cough lasting >3 weeks, coughing up blood or sputum (hemoptysis), night sweats, unexplained weight loss. | Low-grade fever, chest pain during breathing/coughing, loss of appetite, persistent fatigue. | Prolonged contact with active TB cases, immunocompromised status (HIV), overcrowded living conditions. | Massive hemoptysis (coughing up large amounts of blood), severe breathlessness, sudden chest pain. |
| Dengue Fever | A90 | Sudden high fever (104°F/40°C), severe retro-orbital (behind the eyes) headache, joint/muscle pain ("breakbone fever"), maculopapular rash. | Nausea, vomiting, swollen glands, mild bleeding (nosebleeds, bleeding gums), skin bruising. | Mosquito bites (Aedes aegypti/albopictus), urban tropical/subtropical travel or residence. | Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, mucosal bleeding, fluid accumulation, sudden drop in blood pressure (Dengue Hemorrhagic Fever/Shock). |
| Sepsis (Systemic Infection) | A41.9 | High fever or hypothermia (abnormally low body temp), rapid heart rate (tachycardia), rapid breathing (tachypnea), extreme pain/discomfort. | Clammy/sweaty skin, confusion or disorientation, decreased urination, shivering or feeling very cold. | Untreated local infections (UTI, pneumonia, skin wound), recent surgery/hospitalization, compromised immune system. | Systolic blood pressure <90 mmHg, altered mental state, severe shortness of breath, mottled or discolored skin (Septic Shock). |
| Giardiasis | A07.1 | Profuse watery diarrhea, foul-smelling greasy/floating stools, abdominal cramps, severe bloating/gas. | Nausea, loss of appetite, fatigue, weight loss, dehydration, lactose intolerance post-infection. | Ingestion of contaminated water (lakes, streams, shallow wells), poor sanitation, outdoor camping/backpacking. | Severe dehydration, inability to keep fluids down for >24 hours, confusion, high fever. |
| Lyme Disease | A69.20 | Expanding red rash with "bulls-eye" pattern (Erythema migrans), fever, chills, fatigue, body aches, stiff neck. | Joint pain and swelling (especially knees), facial palsy (drooping on one or both sides of face), heart palpitations (Lyme carditis). | Tick bites (Ixodes scapularis/blacklegged tick), outdoor activities in wooded/grassy endemic regions. | Irregular heartbeat/chest pain (Lyme carditis), severe neck stiffness with light sensitivity, acute motor weakness. |
| Amoebiasis (Amoebic Dysentery) | A06.0 | Frequent loose stools containing blood and mucus, severe abdominal cramps, fever, tenesmus (painful urge to defecate). | Weight loss, fatigue, liver tenderness, flatulence, intermittent constipation and diarrhea. | Ingestion of food/water contaminated with Entamoeba histolytica cysts, poor sanitation, travel to developing regions. | Severe right upper quadrant abdominal pain with fever (Amoebic Liver Abscess), peritonitis, profuse rectal bleeding. |
